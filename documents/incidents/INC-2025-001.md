document_type: incident
access_level: internal
created_date: 2025-03-15
---

The payment gateway experienced elevated timeout errors between 09:20 and 10:05 UTC.
The root cause was database connection pool exhaustion in the payment service after a traffic spike.
The support team restored service by increasing the connection pool, restarting unhealthy workers,
and following the payment service recovery runbook.
